# Pattern: Stiffened Sheet-Metal Door

## When useful
Large doors, high gasket/latch loads, equipment mounted on the door, or visible oil-canning/warping.

## Options to compare
- deeper perimeter returns;
- local hat/channel stiffener;
- bonded or welded reinforcement;
- increased thickness;
- more distributed latching.

Do not default to thicker sheet. Compare mass, cost, welding distortion, available bend geometry, coating access, serviceability and gasket compression. For a first-pass stiffness calculation, idealize only clearly beam-like stiffeners; broad sheet panels require plate/FEA or physical verification when deflection matters.
