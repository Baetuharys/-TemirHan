# Market Research Method

Define the market question, geography, time window and company criteria before searching. Build a candidate universe, verify identities and roles, collect primary/secondary evidence, preserve publication dates, and separate confirmed facts from inference. For commercially important claims, seek corroboration when practical. Never turn directory presence or sector fit into a customer/contract claim.
