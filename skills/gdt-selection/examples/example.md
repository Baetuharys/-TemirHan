# Worked example — mounting plate

**Part.** 2 mm steel mounting plate, four Ø6.5 clearance holes for M6 screws on a 100 × 60 mm pattern, plate bolted flat to a frame. ISO 1101.

**Function.** Hole pattern must match the frame; plate face must sit flat.

**Proposed controls.**
| Feature | Characteristic | Tolerance | Datums | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mounting face | Flatness | 0.5 mm | — | thin sheet: non-rigid, state restraint or relax |
| 4 holes Ø6.5 | Position | ⌀0.5 Ⓜ | A, B, C (in this order) | Fixed-fastener check: hole at MMC (min 6.5) ≥ screw max (6.0) + position tolerance (0.5) → 6.5 ≥ 6.5, just OK `[CALCULATED]` |
| Datum A | mounting face | | | datum B/C: two holes or an edge pair, reachable by gauge |

**Process capability check.** Laser holes ±0.1–0.2 mm → ⌀0.5 position zone is realistic `[PRACTICE]`.
**Do not add** a tight flatness on a thin sheet without a function.
**Open:** edition (ISO 1101 / ASME Y14.5) and inspection method.
