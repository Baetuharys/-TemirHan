# Scope — technical-document-generator

## In scope
- Document skeletons, requirement wording, calculation notes
- Open-item handling and review checklist

## Out of scope
- Inventing standards, test results or signatures
- Legal contracts

## Hand-offs
quotation-generator; technical-drawing-review

## Evidence rule
Use the evidence labels of `docs/conventions.md`; mark anything not derived from supplied data, a calculation shown, or a cited source as an assumption.
