# Document structures

**Assembly instruction:** 1 Purpose and scope; 2 Safety and warnings; 3 Parts and tools list (number, name, quantity); 4 Steps (n: title, parts used, action, check, figure); 5 Final inspection; 6 Revision table.
**Work instruction:** purpose, applicability, prerequisites, steps, acceptance criteria, records.
**Technical specification:** scope, normative references, requirements, verification methods, deliverables.
**Inspection report:** item, requirement, method, result, conclusion, signature.
**Design note:** problem, inputs (labelled), method, result, assumptions, open items.
