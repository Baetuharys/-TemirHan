# Worked example — DFM findings

**Part.** 3 mm DC01 bracket, quantity 200, laser + press brake, powder coat.

| # | Location | Class | Finding | Fix | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Flange B, 8 mm | **Error** | Typical minimum flange ≈ 0.7·V; for V = 8·T = 24 mm that is ≈ 17 mm `[PRACTICE]` | Lengthen the flange to ≥ 17 mm or ask the shop for narrow tooling | avoids special tool |
| 2 | Slot 2 mm from bend 1 | Risk | Distance < 2.5·T + R (≈ 7.5 + R) → distortion | Move slot or add after bending | +1 operation if kept |
| 3 | Hole Ø2 mm in 3 mm sheet | Risk | Hole < 1·T laser limit region | Increase to ≥ Ø3 or drill | cost |
| 4 | Radius R0.5 | Risk | Inside radius much smaller than T, cracking risk in stainless/aluminium | Use R ≥ T | — |
| 5 | Coating | Suggestion | Define masked thread and hanging mark locations | add notes | clarity |

**First process that could reject the part:** press brake (finding 1).
**Open:** shop's tooling list, material grade.
