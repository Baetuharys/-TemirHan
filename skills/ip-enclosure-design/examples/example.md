# Worked example — outdoor IP65 control box

**Request.** Outdoor wall-mounted box, hinged door, 4 cable entries, 2 mm sheet, IP65 target.

**Key statements.** IP65 = dust-tight (6) + water jets from any direction (5). It says nothing about corrosion, UV, impact or condensation. Rating is **earned by test**.

| # | Leakage path | Risk | Design response |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Door seam | high | Continuous gasket, hard stop, return flange overlap, corners on a radius |
| 2 | Gasket joint | medium | One splice at a straight edge or moulded/foam-in-place gasket |
| 3 | Welded corners | high | Continuous seams, ground flat where the gasket crosses |
| 4 | Rear mounting holes | high | Mount outside the sealed volume or use sealing washers |
| 5 | Cable entries (4) | high | IP-rated glands matching cable range; unused entries plugged |
| 6 | Lock/handle cut-out | medium | Sealed lock with shaft gasket |
| 7 | Powder coat on gasket face | medium | Controlled thickness, no runs |
| 8 | Heat | — | Heat load needed (W) to decide on sizing/breather |

**Evidence level.** Design intent only → plan a test (own or accredited). Do **not** state "IP65 guaranteed".
**Open items:** gasket datasheet (compression window), gland datasheet, heat load, test plan.
