# DXF Release Check

1. Confirm units and scale.
2. Confirm flat-pattern orientation and revision.
3. Classify every exported geometry group as CUT, MARK, REFERENCE, or OTHER.
4. Confirm the target CAM's process-mapping convention.
5. Inspect the imported DXF in CAM.
6. Confirm marking entities are assigned to the marking process and are not in the cutting operation.
7. Confirm no duplicate/overlapping entities exist.
8. Record the machine/CAM-specific convention in the release note.
