# Worked example — vague enclosure request

**User.** "Dış ortam için sac kabin lazım, IP65 olsun, 2 mm DKP düşünüyorum."

**Intake output.**
- **Task family:** enclosure design with IP target → manufacturing review.
- **Known:** target IP65 · material DKP (DC01), 2 mm `[FACT-SUPPLIED]` · outdoor use.
- **Missing (blocking):** cover/door type (hinged or removable), cable entries (count, cable diameters), heat load inside, overall dimensions.
- **Missing (non-blocking):** finish colour, quantity, lock type.
- **Skill chain:** `ip-enclosure-design → enclosure-gasket-review → sheet-metal-manufacturability-check → technical-drawing-review`.
- **Note:** DKP needs a coating system for outdoor use; stainless or galvanised may be better — decision after exposure details.
- **First questions (max 3):** 1) Kapak menteşeli mi, sökülebilir mi? 2) Kaç kablo girişi ve kablo çapları? 3) İçeride ısı yayan cihaz var mı (W)?
