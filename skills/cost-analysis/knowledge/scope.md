# Scope — cost-analysis

## In scope
- Cost build-up per part and per lot
- Batch/setup effects, nesting yield, scrap
- Markup vs margin
- Sensitivity and top cost drivers
- Design changes that reduce cost

## Out of scope
- Issuing a binding quotation (use quotation-generator)
- Market price research without a source
- Tax/legal advice

## Hand-offs
quotation-generator; sheet-metal-manufacturability-check; weight-analysis

## Evidence rule
Use the evidence labels of `docs/conventions.md`; mark anything not derived from supplied data, a calculation shown, or a cited source as an assumption.
