# Scope — material-property-research

## In scope
- Citing densities, grades and equivalences with source and condition
- Comparing candidate materials for sheet work

## Out of scope
- Certified property values without the mill certificate
- Structural design allowables

## Hand-offs
weight-analysis; sheet-metal-bend-analysis

## Evidence rule
Use the evidence labels of `docs/conventions.md`; mark anything not derived from supplied data, a calculation shown, or a cited source as an assumption.
