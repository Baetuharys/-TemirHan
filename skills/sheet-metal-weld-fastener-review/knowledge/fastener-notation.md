# Fastener notation

`M4 x 15` = metric thread, nominal diameter 4 mm, length 15 mm. See `reference/fasteners-threads.md` for pitch, tap drill and ISO 273 clearance holes.
Length reference: below the head for most head styles; overall length for countersunk. State the standard (ISO 4762, 7380, 4017...).
Self-clinching hardware (PEM-type): hole size, minimum sheet thickness, edge distance and installation force come from the manufacturer's datasheet for the exact part number and sheet material.
Studs welded to sheet mark the opposite face; say which side is cosmetic.
