# Weld notation basics (ISO 2553; verify the standard on the drawing)

- Reference line (solid) + arrow line; a dashed identification line may sit next to the reference line.
- Fillet weld symbol: a triangle. Dimension **a** = throat thickness; **z** = leg length. Writing a or z matters; never leave the letter out.
- Intermittent weld: number of segments x length (distance between segments) written beside the symbol.
- Spot weld: circle symbol; dimensions are weld diameter d and the number of spots n.
- Process numbers (ISO 4063): 111 manual metal arc, 135 MAG, 141 TIG, 21 resistance spot.
- Quality levels: ISO 5817 (B, C, D). General tolerances for welded constructions: ISO 13920.
- Arrow side vs other side decides which face carries the weld.

## Four-corner box weld (design questions)
Continuous or stitched? Is the seam also a seal (IP)? Fit-up gap and corner treatment (open, closed, lapped)? Grinding and finish after welding? Distortion control (tack sequence, fixture)?
