# Example: four-corner sheet-metal weld

Requirement: weld the four corners of a folded sheet-metal assembly.

Review:
- Identify whether the intent is four discrete weld locations or a continuous seam.
- Confirm accessible sides and whether the weld symbol/length notation can express the intent unambiguously.
- If the user says "all four corners", preserve that requirement explicitly in the drawing/release note rather than silently converting it into a continuous weld.
- Determine whether heat input/distortion is relevant to the assembly.
