# Example: M4 x 15

**User:** "M4x15 civatanın çapı kaç?"
- M4 = nominal thread diameter 4 mm (SOURCE-BACKED, fastener designation convention).
- 15 = length 15 mm; measured under the head for most head types (standard-dependent).
- Head diameter and wrench size: NOT in the designation; they depend on the product standard (e.g. ISO 4762 or ISO 7380). Ask which one.
- Hole: tap drill 3.3 mm for coarse thread; clearance hole 4.5 mm (ISO 273 medium).
