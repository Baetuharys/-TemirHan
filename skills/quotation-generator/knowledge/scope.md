# Scope — quotation-generator

## In scope
- Quotation structure, scope, assumptions, exclusions
- Commercial terms and Incoterms
- Technical annex
- Cover email

## Out of scope
- Inventing prices, customers or legal clauses
- Tax/legal advice

## Hand-offs
cost-analysis; technical-document-generator

## Evidence rule
Use the evidence labels of `docs/conventions.md`; mark anything not derived from supplied data, a calculation shown, or a cited source as an assumption.
