# Quotation structure

1. Cover block: customer, project, offer number, date, validity, contact.
2. Scope: part list (number, revision, description, quantity), standards, finish.
3. Price table: item | unit price | quantity | line total | note; currency and VAT status stated.
4. Assumptions and exclusions: materials, finish, packaging, tooling, tests not included.
5. Delivery: lead time from order/drawing approval; delivery terms (state the Incoterms rule and edition if used; check the current edition).
6. Payment terms and validity.
7. Reference documents: drawings with revision, specifications.
8. Acceptance block.

Checklist before sending: every price tagged by source; every term supplied by the user; revisions match; totals recomputed; estimates flagged.
