# Example — Türkiye Sheet-Metal Market Scan

## Request
"Türkiye'de endüstriyel sac kabin, lazer kesim, büküm ve kaynak hizmeti alanında çalışan firmaları; kamuya açık projeleri ve müşterileriyle birlikte araştır. Potansiyel müşterilerimizi ayrıca çıkar."

## Required behavior
- Search current public sources.
- Separate manufacturer/fabricator, contractor, OEM and end customer roles.
- Record source date.
- Do not state an unverified customer relationship as fact.
- Mark inferred potential fit as inference.
- Prefer primary sources for contract/project claims.
- Return a company table plus target-account dossiers.

## Minimum output fields
Company, location, sector, capability, project/signal, relationship type, evidence grade, source date, source, unknowns, potential fit rationale.
