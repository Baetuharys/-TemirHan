# Potential Customer Discovery

1. Define the user's sellable capability: laser, bending, welding, enclosure, assembly, engineering, etc.
2. Define geography and target industries.
3. Search for companies with matching products/processes.
4. Search for current project, expansion, sourcing and procurement signals.
5. Verify the company's role in each signal.
6. Build a target-account dossier.
7. Explain why the account may fit using evidence.
8. List unknowns and a practical verification step.

The output is a research shortlist, not a claim that the company is a customer or intends to buy.
