# Company Market Map

1. Define geography and sector.
2. Collect candidate companies from multiple source classes.
3. Deduplicate names and locations.
4. Verify company identity and role.
5. Capture capabilities and public projects.
6. Record customer/partner relationships only with evidence.
7. Attach dates and source references.
8. Separate confirmed facts from inference.
9. Output a structured market map and evidence gaps.
