# Company Dossier Schema

Minimum record:

- canonical company name
- aliases / brands
- country
- city / facility
- website
- sector / subsector
- manufacturing capabilities
- products / applications
- certifications if publicly documented
- ownership/public-company status when relevant
- projects / contracts / investments
- customer / partner / supplier evidence
- procurement or contact channel
- evidence grade per claim
- source URL/reference per claim
- publication date
- research date
- unknowns / conflicts

Never collapse an unknown field into a guessed value.
