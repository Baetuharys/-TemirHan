# Evidence and Source Hierarchy

## Primary sources
Prefer company, government, procurement, stock-exchange and project-owner sources for claims about contracts, investments, facilities and official relationships.

## Secondary sources
Use reputable trade publications, established media and industry associations to discover and corroborate signals.

## Discovery-only sources
Directories, aggregators, scraped company databases and social posts can identify candidates but should not independently establish a contract, customer or supplier relationship.

## Two-source rule for important claims
For commercially important claims that are not available from a primary source, seek corroboration from another independent source when practical.
