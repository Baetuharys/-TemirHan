# Potential Customer Signal Library

Strong signals:
- public tender or procurement need matching the user's capabilities
- announced plant/facility expansion
- new production line or equipment investment
- new product/program launch requiring fabricated components
- supplier qualification / sourcing announcement
- recurring outsourced fabrication activity documented publicly

Medium signals:
- sector fit plus relevant product portfolio
- hiring for manufacturing/procurement roles related to the capability
- expansion into a product area requiring fabricated metal components

Weak signals:
- generic company-directory category
- social media activity without procurement or project evidence
- sector membership alone

Signal strength is not purchase probability. It only describes the quality of evidence that an account may merit research or outreach.
