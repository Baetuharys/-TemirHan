# Scope — image-dimension-estimation

## In scope
- Scale from a reference object
- Perspective and plane assumptions, error range
- Reporting estimates with confidence

## Out of scope
- Manufacturing dimensions from photos
- Metrology-grade measurement

## Hand-offs
reference-image-analysis; weight-analysis

## Evidence rule
Use the evidence labels of `docs/conventions.md`; mark anything not derived from supplied data, a calculation shown, or a cited source as an assumption.
