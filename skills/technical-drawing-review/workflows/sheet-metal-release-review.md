# Sheet-metal drawing release review
1. Verify material and thickness against the model/release data.
2. Verify bend notes/radii and manufacturing-critical dimensions.
3. Verify weld/fastener callouts and access.
4. Verify title block and revision.
5. Verify flat pattern/DXF relationship where applicable.
6. Check that marking, cut and bend intent is unambiguous downstream.
7. Record findings as BLOCKER / WARNING / OBSERVATION without silently redesigning the part.
