# Gasket design notes

- Seat: flat seat needs a stop or a fixed gap; a groove controls compression by geometry.
- Continuity: run the gasket around the full seal line; corners by radius or a manufacturer-approved joint.
- Flange stiffness: a long thin flange between fasteners lifts; reduce pitch or stiffen the flange (return flange, stiffener).
- Finish: burrs, weld spatter and paint runs on the seat cause leaks.
- Materials: choose by temperature, UV/ozone, oil and chemical exposure; follow the datasheet.
- Conductive gaskets for EMC are a separate selection.
