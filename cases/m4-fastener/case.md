# Case: M4 × 15 Fastener Interpretation

## Scenario
A drawing or BOM contains the notation “M4 × 15”. The user asks for the diameter and what the notation means.

## Known inputs
- Fastener designation: M4 × 15

## Required skill chain
`engineering-task-intake` → `engineering-terminology` → `sheet-metal-weld-fastener-review`

## Required behavior
- Explain what M4 identifies and what 15 normally identifies in a fastener designation.
- Distinguish nominal thread diameter from head geometry and actual measured dimensions.
- State any standard-dependent ambiguity.
