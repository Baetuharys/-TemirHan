## Acceptance criteria
- Density is an explicit input with provenance/status.
- Mass calculation relationship is shown: mass = volume × density.
- Units are consistent.
- Any missing temper/product-form detail is flagged.
- No unsupported density or mechanical property is presented as an exact fact.

## Verification
- Compare CAD volume with model configuration.
- Confirm material assignment/configuration.
- Recalculate after any material or geometry change.
