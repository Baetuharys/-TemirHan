# Case: Four-Corner Sheet-Metal Weld Review

## Scenario
Four locations on a sheet-metal assembly require welding. The drawing needs an unambiguous manufacturing indication.

## Known inputs
- Four weld locations are required.
- Sheet-metal assembly.
- Exact weld process and joint geometry are not yet supplied.

## Required skill chain
`sheet-metal-weld-fastener-review` → `technical-drawing-review`

## Required behavior
- Determine joint type and weld intent before selecting a symbol/detail.
- Distinguish “weld at all four locations” from a generic all-around symbol.
- Request or identify missing process/size/length information when it materially affects the instruction.
- Keep the drawing instruction manufacturable and inspectable.
