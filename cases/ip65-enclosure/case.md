# Case: IP65 Sheet-Metal Enclosure

## Scenario
An industrial sheet-metal enclosure is required to meet an IP65 target. The design includes a removable cover, gasket, cable entries, ventilation considerations, and drainage questions.

## Known inputs
- Target: IP65
- Construction: sheet-metal enclosure
- Cover: removable
- Environmental details: incomplete

## Required skill chain
`ip-enclosure-design` → `enclosure-gasket-review` → `sheet-metal-manufacturability-check` → `technical-drawing-review`

## Required behavior
- Translate the IP target into design interfaces that require attention.
- Treat cable glands, vents, doors, seams, fasteners, gasket compression, and service openings as potential leakage paths.
- Distinguish design intent from certification/test evidence.
- Do not claim compliance solely from geometry.
